You’ll discuss with three AI participants: an aggressive debater, a quiet analyst and a data-driven moderate. Open the discussion, then respond to them.

JAM rules of thumb

  • Start within 3 seconds: a clear opening line, not “umm, so…”.
  • Use a simple structure: hook, two or three points, a closing line.
  • Never stop. If you lose the thread, pivot to a related angle.
  • Avoid repetition, hesitation and fillers (“basically”, “like”, “you know”).
  • Aim for about 120–150 words in 60 seconds.

GD rules of thumb

  • Initiate with a definition, a fact or a framing, within the first minute if you can.
  • Quality over quantity: 3–4 well-argued interventions beat constant interruption.
  • Reference others (“Building on Priya’s point…”) and add something new.
  • Use data, examples and structure (economic, social, ethical angles).
  • Rescue a stalled group, and summarise if the discussion is drifting.
  • Disagree with the idea, never the person.
